**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Want to play cricket this evening but don't have enough players? Looking for a running group, a weekend trek or a badminton partner nearby? Meelo connects you with people around you who want to do the same thing — today.

🗺️ DISCOVER WHAT'S HAPPENING NEARBY
See activities around you on a live map — cricket, football, running, badminton, trekking, yoga, meetups and more. Filter by distance (2 km to 50 km) and by category, and see what's starting soon.

➕ CREATE AN ACTIVITY IN SECONDS
Pick a sport, choose a place on the map, set the date, time and how many players you need. Your activity is instantly visible to everyone nearby.

🙋 JOIN WITH ONE TAP
Found something you like? Tap Join and you're in. See who else is coming and how many spots are left.

💬 GROUP CHAT FOR EVERY ACTIVITY
Each activity has its own group chat, so you can plan the details — who's bringing the ball, where exactly to meet.

🔔 STAY IN THE LOOP
Get notified when someone joins your activity or when plans change.

📤 SHARE ON WHATSAPP
Share any activity with friends in one tap. They'll see the time, place and spots left right in the link preview.

Meelo is free. Browse activities without an account — sign in with Google or email when you're ready to join or create.

Questions or feedback? Write to support@meelo.online
```
